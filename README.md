# Delay Defence Force 🛡️🚂

The 8:15 is late. Again. Boredom is shuffling down the carriage toward your seat.
Place defences in the seat spaces, shush the bores directly, and survive as many
delayed waves as your sanity allows.

A one-page, zero-dependency, offline-capable web game (PWA). No build step, no
npm, no assets — just emoji, numbers going up, and consequences.

## Play locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4141
# open http://localhost:4141
```

(Any static server works; the service worker needs HTTP(S), so don't open
`index.html` straight from `file://` — Safari will ignore offline caching.)

## Put it on your iPhone (offline play)

1. Serve the folder once over **HTTPS** — easiest free options:
   - **Netlify Drop**: drag the folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop
   - **GitHub Pages**: push the folder to a repo, enable Pages
2. On the iPhone, open the URL in **Safari** (not Chrome — iOS ignores every
   other browser engine).
3. Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Launch it from the home screen. After that first load it works with
   **airplane mode on** — the service worker caches everything, the game state
   lives in localStorage, and nothing phones home (it can't, there's no server).

Notes:
- iOS may clear website data after long disuse for *browser tabs*, but
  home-screen apps are treated like installed apps — your run is safe.
- Your run auto-saves at every wave boundary and on closing the app. A wave in
  progress is never saved — you resume at the intermission before it.

## The rules

| | |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | Stop bores from shuffling 9 seat spaces down the carriage to your seat 🧍. Each one that makes it costs 🧠 sanity; 10 leaks = commute lost. |
| **Money** | 💰 from kills, wave-clear bonuses, early-start bonuses and the occasional 🧁. |
| **Waves** | Infinite, escalating. Every 5th wave sends a 🧳 Luggage Bloke. |
| **Tapping** | Tap a bore directly to shush it — small damage, no cooldown cost, works every wave. |
| **Slows** | 🍿 and 🧘 slow bores; only the strongest slow applies (no stacking). |

The defends:

| Emoji | Name | Cost | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥨 | Snack Vendor | 25 | 2.5 dps, short range. Cheap body filler. |
| 🧩 | Crossword Kid | 60 | 6 dps, medium range. |
| 🍿 | Popcorn Lady | 100 | slows 40%, tiny dps |
| 🎧 | Portable DJ | 140 | 11 dps, long range |
| 🧘 | Yoga Aunt | 220 | slows 65%, longest range |

Every defence upgrades to level 4 (×~1.8 more each level) and sells for half of
what went into it.
